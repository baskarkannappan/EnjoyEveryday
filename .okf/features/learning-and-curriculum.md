# Learning, Curriculum & Experience Runner

## Architectural Overview
EnjoyEveryday provides an activity runner and pedagogical curriculum framework rooted in early childhood learning domains (Cognitive, Social-Emotional, Physical, Language, Creative Arts).

## Key Services (`EnjoyEveryday.Application.Services`)
* **`PlannerService`**: Generates and stores weekly draft planning items, binds activities to classrooms and age tiers. Supports scheduling multiple experiences throughout operating hours (8:00 AM – 7:00 PM) across categories (`Morning`, `Mid-Day`, `Afternoon`, `Evening`) with exact time-window collision detection (`[startA, endA)` vs `[startB, endB)`). Enforces `IsDaycareClosedForPlanning` so experiences cannot be scheduled, updated, or completed on closed daycare days (weekends, past dates, or after closing `>= 7:00 PM`), with support for test override via `Bypass_daycare_closed`. In `PlanExperienceDialog`, real-time slot conflict warning displays existing classroom sessions and validates time overlaps dynamically.
* **`ExecutionService`**: Manages live `ExperienceSession` states (`Scheduled`, `InProgress`, `Completed`) and participant attendance (`ExperienceParticipation`). Enforces `IsDaycareClosedForActivity` in `StartExperienceAsync`, `CompleteExperienceAsync`, and `CompleteExperienceWithParticipationsAsync` so starting or ending any activity is prohibited whenever the daycare is closed (weekends, outside 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM operating hours, non-current dates, or after End-of-Day closure).
* **`IMemoryService` / `MemoryService`**: Queries `public.fn_get_child_memories` via `MemoryRepository` with server-side pagination (`Limit = 11`, `Offset = (_currentPage - 1) * 10`) to display 10 records per page with Previous/Next navigation and avoid loading large base64 evidence payloads all at once.
* **`IChildJourneyService`**: Logs developmental milestone progress linked to EYFS / Early Learning Standards.
  - **Session-to-Journey Integration**: `ActiveSessionModal.razor` includes a guided Step 2 review allowing educators to select participating children, pre-fill notes from session reflections, and publish moments with `PendingReview` moderation.
  - **Moderation Workflow**: `sp_approve_journey_entry` enables lead teachers and directors to review and approve child moments (`parent_visibility_status = 'Approved'`, `is_parent_visible = true`) before making them visible in the Parent Portal.
  - **Deduplication Guard**: `fn_has_journey_entry_for_participation` ensures an activity session participation cannot be duplicated in a child's developmental timeline.
* **`StandaloneHtmlGenerator` & `StorybookStorageService`**: Generates and caches visual pedagogical storybooks at `/api/experiences/{id}/storybook` dynamically incorporating execution phases, inquiry wonder prompts, and spoken scripts without static craft boilerplate. Supports 6-panel Pixar-style illustrated picture books via Gemini master prompts (`/api/experiences/{id}/storybook/prompt`), image uploads (`/api/experiences/{id}/storybook/image`), in-page responsive Lightbox modal viewer with safe new-tab opening and image download, and professional multi-step `@media print` formatting that eliminates blank pages and renders the complete manual with all instructional chapters.
* **`IStorybookGenerationService`**: Encapsulates `BuildGeminiStorybookPrompt` formatting 6-panel 2x3 grid prompts for Google Gemini web generation.
* **`AiIdeaService` (`http://127.0.0.1:8000`)**: Orchestrates Gemini (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite`) for idea generation (`/api/ideas`) and pedagogical improvements (`/api/improve`), with classroom grounding options (`IdeaGenerationOptions`), few-shot tenant exemplar experience integration, pediatric safety validation, resilient failover, structured metadata (`AiExecutionMeta`), and live streaming diagnostic logging (`AiIdeaDiagnostics`) for studio enhancements.
* **`ChildJourneyInsightService` & `ChildJourneyInsightPanel`**: Generates grounded developmental insights from the SQL Memory Layer using 4-domain memory clustering (Communication, Social-Emotional, Physical-Motor, Cognitive Inquiry), PII de-identification, and Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) trajectory modeling with 1-click scheduling (`/planning?experienceId=...&classroomId=...&childId=...`). `ExperiencePlanning.razor` binds `[SupplyParameterFromQuery]`, selects the target classroom, surfaces a prominent recommendation banner, and auto-opens `PlanExperienceDialog` with the suggested experience pre-selected and badged.
* **`ExperienceMarketplaceService` (`/marketplace`)**: Manages platform curated curriculum, entitlements, and import to private organization libraries. Enforces strict organization-wide duplicate prevention via `uq_experiences_org_platform_exp` and pre-import checks so an experience can be imported only once per organization across all branches and users. Marketplace cards dynamically display `✓ In Library` badges and `View in Library` navigation when already imported.
* **Organization Experience Library Delete / Discard (`Experiences.razor`, `ExperienceCard.razor`, `ExperienceService.DeleteExperienceAsync`)**: Deleting/discarding experiences (whether studio-created or marketplace-acquired) is restricted strictly to **Org Admin** and **Branch Admin** (`CanDeleteExperiences()` in `ExperienceService`, `_canDeleteExperiences` in `Experiences.razor`), excluding Lead Teachers/Teachers. Confirmation modal invokes `sp_delete_experience` (soft-archiving to `Archived` so historical classroom session/planning records remain intact).



* **Platform Curation (Harvest from Daycare)**: PlatformCurationPage.razor (Admin portal `/platform-curation`) lists ALL active organizations via `IPlatformAdminRepository.GetOrganizationsOverviewAsync()` (each org owns its own `tenant_id`, so a tenant-scoped `OrgRepo.GetAllAsync` hid every org outside the admin's tenant). Source experiences are read with the selected org's TenantId, and `ExperienceCurationService.InitiatePlatformHarvestAsync(..., sourceTenantId)` stores the harvest request under the source org's tenant so `ReviewHarvestRequestAsync` can resolve the source experience. Marketplace-acquired experiences (`PlatformExperienceId != null`) are filtered out from the harvest picker and blocked server-side in `ExperienceCurationService` (`EnsureEligibleForHarvest`) so the platform cannot re-acquire its own published experiences.
* **Full-Detail & Asset Preservation during Harvesting & Publishing**:
  * **Instructional Media & Photos**: `ExperienceCurationService.SanitizePayloadForPlatform` cleanses individual student/child PII (`childname`, `studentname`, `childid`, `familyname`, `privateobservation`, `attendancelog`) while preserving all curriculum instructional media, activity photos, illustrations, materials, and storybook assets.
  * **Curriculum Metadata**: Harvesting automatically parses `ExperienceDna` into canonical database columns: `age_range_min_months`, `age_range_max_months`, `duration_minutes`, `learning_objectives`, `required_materials`, `instructions`, `safety_notes`, and auto-resolves `creator_attribution` from the source organization's name.
  * **Storybook & Grid Cover Image Propagation**: When platform admins approve and curate an experience into `platform_experiences`, `TryCopyStorybookAssets` copies storybook HTML templates and cover grid images. When other daycares acquire or import the experience via `ExperienceMarketplaceService.ImportToOrganizationLibraryAsync`, these assets are seamlessly cloned to the newly minted organization-owned experience.
* **Platform Marketplace Origin Differentiation, Discontinue & Discard (`PlatformMarketplacePage.razor`)**:
  * **Origin & Source Org Visibility**: `PlatformMarketplacePage.razor` (`/platform-marketplace`) resolves `SourceOrganizationId` via `IPlatformAdminRepository.GetOrganizationsOverviewAsync()`, displays `Acquired · <Org Name>` vs `Platform Created` origin badges on each card, and provides origin filter tabs (`All Sources`, `Acquired from Orgs`, `Platform Created`) plus a `Source Org` dropdown alongside status tabs (`All`, `Published`, `Drafts`, `Suspended`, `Discontinued`).
  * **Discontinue & Discard Lifecycle**: `ExperienceCurationService.RetireMarketplaceItemAsync` transitions items to `PlatformPublicationStatus.Retired` (removing them from the daycare catalog while preserving existing organization entitlements). `ExperienceCurationService.DiscardPlatformExperienceAsync` (backed by `IExperienceMarketplaceRepository.DeletePlatformExperienceAsync` and `fn_delete_platform_experience` in migration `130_platform_experience_delete_function.sql`) permanently deletes unpurchased platform experiences and cleans up copied storybook files, while blocking deletion if `marketplace_orders` or `organization_entitlements` exist.

* **Type-Safe Learning & Audit Constants (`EnjoyEveryday.Domain.Constants.LearningConstants`, `EnjoyEveryday.Shared.Audit.AuditConstants`)**:
  * **Journey Types**: `JourneyTypes` (`Observation`, `ActivityObservation`, `LittleMoment`, `Discovery`, `Milestone`, `Attendance`, `Art & Sensory`).
  * **Parent Visibility Review**: `ParentVisibilityStatuses` (`Draft`, `PendingReview`, `Approved`).
  * **Participation Modes**: `ParticipationTypes` (`Participated`, `Observed`, `Assisted`).
  * **Structured Audit Logging**: `AuditActions.Journey` (`Created`, `Updated`, `Approved`, `MilestoneCaptured`) and `AuditEntityTypes` (`ChildJourneyEntry`, `ChildMilestone`). All stored procedure and Dapper mappings maintain 100% backward compatibility with PostgreSQL `varchar` columns.
