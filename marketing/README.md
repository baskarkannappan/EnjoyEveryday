1. **Discovery (Minutes 0–4):**

   - *"How do your educators currently record routines, meals, and naps?"*
2. **The "Ah-Ha" Product Showcase (Minutes 4–11):**
   - **Show 1: Daycare Web Organization Dashboard (`https://localhost:7003/`):** Point to the live ratio compliance and pending attendance confirmations.
   - **Show 2: Multi-Campus Hierarchy (`/organization/hierarchy`):** Expand branches (Downtown, Midtown, Uptown) showing capacity and staff counts.
   - **Show 3: Parent Portal (`https://localhost:7005/`):** Show the serene parent view: verified check-in, 1-tap absence reporting, and daily moments feed.
3. **Closing & Next Step (Minutes 11–15):**
   - *"We handle 100% of your roster and data migration for free within 48 hours."*
   - *"Would you like to start a 14-day trial in your rooms starting this Monday?"*
