Child information is accessible only when the user, relationship, purpose and authorization permit access.
Consent

AI Governance
AI recommendations cannot bypass application validation, authorization, consent or safety rules.
Accountability
Protected actions are recorded with identity, purpose, timestamp and relevant context.
AI Safety Rules
AI does not make safety decisions
AI can identify possibilities and provide recommendations. Safety rules remain authoritative.
AI cannot invent reality
AI cannot create fictional observations, child events, accomplishments or memories.
AI cannot bypass access controls
Every AI request operates within the user's authorization, tenant boundary and purpose.
Human decision remains required
Recommendations become application actions only after validation and, where required, human approval.
