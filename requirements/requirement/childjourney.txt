# EnjoyEveryday — Child Journey Requirements

## 1. Purpose

The **Child Journey** feature captures and presents a child's development over time through meaningful observations, activities, little moments, milestones, and teacher insights.

The goal is not to create a traditional assessment or scorecard.

The goal is to answer:

> **"How is this child growing, learning, expressing themselves, and becoming more independent over time?"**

Child Journey should create a continuous story from everyday classroom experiences.

### Core principle

```text
Teacher does an activity
        ↓
Child participates
        ↓
Teacher notices something
        ↓
Teacher records a quick observation
        ↓
Journey grows
        ↓
Parent sees meaningful progress
```

The teacher should be able to record a journey moment in **30–60 seconds**.

---

# 2. Product Philosophy

Child Journey must feel:

* Warm
* Positive
* Human
* Simple
* Evidence-based
* Child-centered
* Non-judgmental
* Development-focused

Avoid turning the feature into:

* A school grading system
* A leaderboard
* A numerical child score
* A complex teacher assessment form
* A medical diagnosis system
* A performance ranking system

Do not expose arbitrary percentages such as:

```text
Communication: 78%
Social: 64%
```

Instead, present meaningful statements such as:

```text
Maya is finding her voice.

Maya is becoming more independent.

Maya enjoys creating and exploring.

Maya is learning to collaborate with friends.
```

These statements must be derived from recorded evidence and should never invent facts.

---

# 3. Child Journey Concepts

The domain contains the following concepts:

### 3.1 Journey Entry

A meaningful observation about a child.

Example:

> Maya built a tower independently today.

### 3.2 Little Moment

A small memorable experience.

Example:

> Maya laughed when the bubbles floated onto her nose.

### 3.3 Milestone

A significant developmental or personal achievement.

Example:

> Maya used scissors independently for the first time.

### 3.4 Activity Evidence

Evidence generated from a classroom activity.

Example:

```text
Activity:
Painting Butterflies

Child:
Maya

Observation:
Maya mixed two colors and explained what happened.
```

### 3.5 Development Area

The area associated with the observation.

Examples:

* Social
* Emotional
* Communication
* Language
* Cognitive
* Physical
* Creative
* Collaboration
* Independence
* Understanding

# 11. Child Voice

Where appropriate, allow the teacher to capture the child's own words.

Example:

```text

"Look! I made a rainbow."
```

```text

```

# 12. Photo and Media

Journey entries may contain photos or videos.

Reuse the existing media system.

Do not create a second media-storage implementation.

Media should reference:

```text
MediaId
```

and contain visibility rules.

Supported visibility:

```text
InternalOnly
Classroom
Parent
SelectedParents
Public
```

Default should be:

```text
InternalOnly
```

unless configured otherwise.

### Important privacy rule

A classroom-level media consent does not automatically mean every child in the photo has consent.

The system must respect the individual child's media/consent rules.

# 16. Growth Themes

The system may identify recurring themes from evidence.

Example:

```text
Maya is finding her voice.

Recent observations show Maya increasingly:

• Shares ideas with the group
• Tells short stories
• Answers questions
• Talks with friends
```

Another example:

```text
Maya is becoming more independent.

Recent observations include:

• Cleaning up independently
• Choosing activities herself
• Putting materials away
• Completing tasks with less support
```

These themes should be evidence-based.

AI may assist with summarization.

AI must not:

* Diagnose a child
* Assign developmental disorders
* Make medical conclusions
* Invent observations
* Invent milestones
* Override teacher observations
* Automatically publish AI-generated content to parents

# 21. Multi-Child Observation

One classroom moment may involve multiple children.

Example:

```text
During block play:

Maya built the tower.
Noah helped her.
Ava explained the colors.
```

The system should allow:

```text
Create classroom moment
        ↓
Select children
        ↓
Create individual journey entries
```

Each child's journey entry should remain independently permissioned.

Do not expose one child's private observation to another child's parent.

# 25. Teacher Permissions

### Teacher

Can:

* View assigned children's journeys
* Create journey entries
* Add observations
* Add evidence
* Record milestones
* Edit own entries
* View relevant classroom journey

### Lead Teacher

Can additionally:

* Review classroom journey
* Review teacher observations
* Approve parent-facing journey summaries
* Create classroom growth summaries

### Director

Can:

* View journeys within permitted branch/classrooms
* Configure development areas
* Configure journey settings
* Review reports

### Admin

Full access subject to tenant/security rules.

# 26. Privacy and Security

Child Journey contains sensitive child information.

Implement:

```text
Tenant isolation
Branch authorization
Classroom authorization
Child authorization
Role-based access
Audit logging
Media access control
Parent visibility control
```

Teachers must not be able to access children outside their authorized scope.

Parents must only see their own child's approved journey.

Never place sensitive child information in:

* Logs
* URLs
* Browser console output
* Error messages
* AI prompts without appropriate controls
* Analytics events

# 28. Deletion Rules

Do not immediately hard-delete journey evidence that may be required for historical or legal reasons.

Use appropriate lifecycle rules.

For normal user deletion:

```text
Draft → Deleted
```

For published historical records:

```text
Active → Archived
```

Any permanent deletion must respect the system's retention policy.

# 38. Acceptance Criteria

The feature is complete when:

1. A teacher can open an authorized child's Journey.
2. A teacher can create a journey entry in less than one minute.
3. A journey entry can be connected to an activity.
4. A teacher can select multiple development areas.
5. A teacher can attach approved media.
6. A teacher can record a child quote.
7. A teacher can create a milestone.
8. Journey entries appear chronologically.
9. Journey can be filtered by date and development area.
10. Multiple children can be associated with a classroom moment.
11. Each child's journey remains independently secured.
12. Parents see only approved/visible journey information.
13. Internal teacher notes are never exposed to parents.
14. Existing Child, Teacher, Classroom, Activity, Media, Consent, and Audit domains are reused.
15. Tenant isolation is enforced.
16. Authorization is enforced at the service/API layer.
17. Important changes are audited.
18. Journey history is preserved.
19. No numerical developmental score is required.
20. AI-generated content cannot automatically become an approved parent-facing record.
21. Teachers remain the final authority over observations and parent-facing summaries.
22. The UI works on desktop and mobile.
23. The feature does not require teachers to duplicate activity information.
24. A completed activity can quickly produce journey observations.
25. The child's journey creates a meaningful chronological story of growth.

---

# 39. Product Success Measure

The most important success metric is not:

```text
How many forms did teachers complete?
```

It is:

```text
How easily can a teacher capture something meaningful
about a child during the normal classroom day?
```

The desired experience is:

> **See something → capture it → child journey grows.**

The final product should make the teacher feel:

> "I am documenting the child without doing paperwork."

And make the parent feel:

> "I can actually see my child's everyday growth."

---

# 40. Final Product Principle

Child Journey is not an assessment database.

It is the child's **living story**.

```text
Everyday activity
       ↓
Little moment
       ↓
Teacher observation
       ↓
Evidence
       ↓
Development
       ↓
Milestone
       ↓
Journey
       ↓
Parent story
       ↓
Child's history
```

The system should quietly collect these moments over months and years so that the child's journey becomes one of the most valuable records in EnjoyEveryday.
